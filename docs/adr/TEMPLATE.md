## Context

What forces are at play? Constraints, requirements, and what makes this decision necessary now.
Include the options that were realistically considered.

## Decision

The change we are making, stated in one or two sentences, followed by the specifics (modules, types,
configuration, migration path).

## Consequences

**Positive:** what becomes easier.

**Negative / costs:** what becomes harder, what is now constrained, what must be maintained.

**Follow-ups:** work this decision creates or defers.
